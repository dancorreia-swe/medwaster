# MedWaster / EduConecta

Educational platform where students complete trails and articles about safe, sustainable healthcare waste handling, and earn a certificate when they finish.

## Certificates

**Certificate**:
The credential a student earns after meeting the unlock requirement, reviewed (or auto-approved) before it is issued as a PDF.

**Certificate Design**:
The single, platform-wide visual configuration applied when a certificate PDF is rendered. There is exactly one at any time.
_Avoid_: Template, theme, certificate settings

**Layout**:
One of the built-in structural starting points a Certificate Design is based on: Clássico, Moderno, or Minimalista. Admins pick a Layout; they do not create new ones.
_Avoid_: Template, preset

**Palette**:
One of the built-in, contrast-checked color combinations (ink, accent, background tint) a Certificate Design uses. EduConecta is the default. Admins pick a Palette; they do not choose arbitrary colors.
_Avoid_: Theme, color scheme, colors

**Optional Element**:
A piece of certificate content the admin can show or hide: student photo, average score, completed count, study time, QR code, footer slogan. The student name, title, achievement sentence, completion date, and verification code are never optional.
_Avoid_: Field, section, block
